# Billing Policy

## Double Charges and Pending Holds
- If a customer is billed twice for a single transaction, the system automatically detects duplicate authorizations.
- Duplicate charges are reversed automatically within 3 to 5 business days depending on the customer's financial institution.
- Support agents can issue an immediate refund authorization if requested.

## Invoices and Payment Methods
- Receipts are sent automatically to the account email.
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal.
