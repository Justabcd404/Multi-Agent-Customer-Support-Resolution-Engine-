# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Order Tracking
- All orders include a tracking number upon dispatch via FedEx or UPS.
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments
- If tracking shows no updates for over 48 hours, contact carrier support or notify our operations team.
- Orders marked delivered but not received can be investigated for reshipment within 14 days of marked delivery date.
